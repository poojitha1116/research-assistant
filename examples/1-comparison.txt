QUESTION: Should a computer science student learn PyTorch or JAX first for ML research?
------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY
PyTorch is widely recommended as the primary starting point for machine learning students due to its dominant research ecosystem, extensive community support, and ease of use. JAX is a high-performance alternative favored for specialized research and TPU-heavy workloads but generally carries a steeper learning curve.

KEY POINTS
 - PyTorch has achieved near-hegemony in research, powering over 70% of papers on arXiv. [2]
 - PyTorch offers a dynamic computational graph that is intuitive for debugging and experimenting. [1, 2]
 - JAX is a high-performance framework based on functional programming and XLA, well-suited for scalable research. [1, 5]
 - The Hugging Face ecosystem, which is PyTorch-first, provides a massive collection of pre-trained models that facilitate the learning process. [2, 3]

CAVEATS
 - While PyTorch is considered the industry and research standard, the field evolves rapidly; some sources note JAX is increasingly adopted for high-performance research.

RECOMMENDATION
Start with PyTorch due to its vast community, abundance of learning materials, and integration with the Hugging Face ecosystem, then consider JAX if your specific research requirements demand its high-performance functional programming capabilities.
